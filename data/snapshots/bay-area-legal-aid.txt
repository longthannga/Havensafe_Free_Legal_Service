When the LAL is open you may request a call-back if you do not want to wait on hold for an attorney to become available to answer your call. Calls are answered and called back in the order they are received, so you will not lose your place in line if you choose the call-back option. We will call you back by the close of the same business day. Return calls come from a blocked number in the interest of client safety. Please keep this in mind and make sure that your phone accepts blocked calls. Because we may close early due to high call volume, it is best to call right when we open at 9:30A.M.
